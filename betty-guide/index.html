Who is Betty?

Betty is the WeBetAI face inside Grok Bot. She brings free daily Omega sportsbook picks into a chat you already open. No new app. No paid hard-sell in the MVP.

You find her on X from @WeBetSocialAI, land on webetsocial.com/grokbot, tap Add, and she starts talking. Video hello. Today's card. WHY when you ask. SCORE when games tip. Finals when they wrap.

This page is the story of that loop, with X-style posts and Grok Bot chat frames built from real Betty art.
