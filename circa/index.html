How WeBetAI Plays This Contest
  1. The objective is 1st place, not profit. Contest scoring has no juice, so breakeven is 50 percent, but winning the Circa Million has historically taken roughly 66 percent ATS across 90 picks. The model therefore optimizes probability of topping the leaderboard, which is a different math problem than maximizing dollar EV at a sportsbook.
  2. Scan the full board, rank by calibrated cover probability. Every game gets a projected margin from the same engine that powers the NFL and CFB pick environments. The gap between projection and spread converts to a cover probability through a sport-specific margin distribution, then gets shrunk toward the market: the contest rewards the 5 highest true probabilities, not the 5 biggest raw gaps.
  3. Key numbers are contest gold. Roughly 15 percent of NFL games land exactly on 3 or 7. A pick sitting on the right side of a key number converts a slice of losses into pushes, and each push banks a guaranteed 0.5 points. Getting +3 instead of +2.5 is worth far more in contest points than the half point suggests, so the model pays a premium for full key numbers (3, 7, 10, and 14 in college).
  4. Leverage beats consensus. Thousands of entries cluster on the same popular sides. Matching the field wins nothing: when the chalk covers, everyone scores together. A card that is right where the field is wrong gains full points of separation, so when public concentration is heavy on one side and the model is neutral or opposed, the card takes the contrarian number. Controlled variance raises the probability of finishing 1st even when it slightly lowers the expected point total.
  5. Use the whole clock. Lines post Thursday 10 AM PT and lock Saturday 4 PM PT. The model re-runs daily inside that window and submits near the deadline to price in injury news, weather, and line movement. Thursday games are avoided by default: rule 8 forces all 5 picks in before the earliest selected kickoff, and surrendering two days of information on four other picks is rarely worth one early edge.
  6. Manage the season like a tournament. KPIs above track points pace against the 66 percent winning benchmark. Behind pace, the card tilts toward higher-leverage contrarian picks to create catch-up variance. Ahead of pace, it tilts toward the highest-probability sides to defend position. The quarterly prize structure (awards every 4 to 5 weeks) gets the same treatment inside each quarter.
  7. College is the sharper edge, same math. Circa's open contests are NFL only, so the CFB card is WeBetAI's application of the identical format to the college board. College spreads are softer, variance is wider, and key numbers hit harder, which is exactly the environment where disciplined cover-probability ranking gains the most over a public field.
