Contest scoring has no juice. How WeBetAI Plays This Contest
  1. The objective is 1st place, not profit. Contest scoring has no juice, so breakeven is 50 percent, but winning the Circa Million has historically taken roughly 66 percent ATS across 90 picks. The model therefore optimizes probability of topping the leaderboard, which is a different math problem than maximizing dollar EV at a sportsbook.
  2. Scan the full NFL board, rank by calibrated cover probability. Every game gets a projected margin from the same engine that powers the NFL pick environment. The gap between projection and spread converts to a cover probability through an NFL margin distribution (extra mass at 3 and 7), then gets shrunk toward the market: the contest rewards the 5 highest true probabilities, not the 5 biggest raw gaps.
  3. Key numbers are contest gold. Roughly 15 percent of NFL games land exactly on 3 or 7. A pick sitting on the right side of a key number converts a slice of losses into pushes, and each push banks a guaranteed 0.5 points. Getting +3 instead of +2.5 is worth far more in contest points than the half point suggests, so the model pays a premium for full key numbers (3, 7, 10, and 14).
  4. Leverage beats consensus. Thousands of entries cluster on the same popular sides. Matching the field wins nothing: when the chalk covers, everyone scores together. A card that is right where the field is wrong gains full points of separation, so when public concentration is heavy on one side and the model is neutral or opposed, the card takes the contrarian number. Controlled variance raises the probability of finishing 1st even when it slightly lowers the expected point total.
  5. Use the whole clock. Contest spreads post Thursday 10:00 AM PT and lock Saturday 4:00 PM PT. Autopilot writes a first card after the sheet posts (~10:15 AM PT Thursday), refreshes Friday morning, and locks a final card Saturday 1:00 PM PT — always against the same frozen Circa PDF while the live market moves. Thursday games are avoided by default: rule 8 says an early kickoff forces all 5 picks in before that game, and surrendering two days of information on four other picks is rarely worth one early edge unless the cover-probability gap is large.
  6. Manage the season like a tournament. KPIs above track points pace against the 66 percent winning benchmark. Behind pace, the card tilts toward higher-leverage contrarian picks to create catch-up variance. Ahead of pace, it tilts toward the highest-probability sides to defend position. The quarterly prize structure (Weeks 1–4, 5–9, 10–13, 14–18) gets the same treatment inside each quarter.
  7. Only the contest PDF is the pick line. Circa Million grades against the frozen weekly Contest Point Spreads PDF, not a shopped retail number.
