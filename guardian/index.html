The Mental Health Challenge
Information overload is real. The average person scrolls through 300 feet of content per day. Most of it is noise. Some of it is deliberately misleading. And the mental toll — anxiety, distrust, isolation — compounds silently.
The irony of social media is that it was built to connect us, but the echo chamber effect often leaves us more isolated, more anxious, and less confident in what we actually know. Not because the platforms are broken — but because we're consuming without a filter for what's real.
The missing piece isn't less information — it's authentic information. When you know what's real, you feel confident enough to talk about it. When you talk about it, you connect with people. When you connect with people, everything else gets better.
