Design Rules
One fair price: Spreads, totals, and moneylines share a paired no-vig anchor. The Edge figure is the shrink-adjusted gap versus that price.
Engines fail soft: A missing starter, EPA row, or goalie rate adds zero. It does not blank the slate and it does not invent the missing number.
Short cards are allowed: Three straights is a maximum. Steam and placeability can leave the card empty. Verify does not refill it.
Observers do not publish: Walk-forward fit is off. Samples from September 22, 2026 forward are stored for a later review. Generate does not read them.
Caps live in code: 3.5 units across straights, 0.5 on the parlay, 4.0 for the card, and 1.25 on any one straight. Quarter-Kelly sizes the straight before those caps.
Walk-Forward Observer
Two jobs record lock-to-close samples for dates on or after September 22, 2026. They refresh yesterday and today. They do not edit the published card, and they do not post a grade into the next run.
Morning Writes the day’s samples and a report after the card is up. A game that has not closed yet keeps a null close.
Evening Runs after the close pass and writes the same store again. Still write-only.
Fit stays off No coefficient, shrink map, or gate from those samples is applied. The calibrator keeps the fixed constants already on the card.
