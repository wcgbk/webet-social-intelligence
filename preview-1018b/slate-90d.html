How often is "3 straights + a parlay" realistically supported?

Games available is not the binding constraint: 77 of 90 days have 12+ games (all sports), 83 have 8+ and only 2 have fewer than 4. The binding constraint is how many sides clear the push-aware EV ≥2% gate at the bet price. Evidence from the 58 priced backtest days (2025-10-07 onward, per-day best side per game, live 6d22a14 vs the preview branch):

By slate size (live code, gate-clearing straights ≥3): ≤3 games 0 of 11 days, 4-7 games 0 of 15, 8-11 games 2 of 15 (13%), 12+ games 6 of 17 (35%). With the fill rule, ≥3 straights incl. leans was reached on 88% of 12+ game days and 73% of 8-11 game days.

Reading for the next 90 days: with 12+ games on 77 of 90 days (NHL + NBA + NCAAF Saturdays + NFL Sundays), a real 3-straights + both-EV-positive parlay is supported on roughly 25-35% of days; a 3-straight card with leans on ~85-90%; a 2-leg lean parlay as the fallback on nearly every day that has ≥2 straights. Lean fills are 0.25u and carry EV near 0, so they add pick count, not profit.

Caveats: (1) ESPN lists only games it has scheduled; NFL playoffs (start ~1/16) are outside the 90 days, CFP semifinals and some bowl games and all remaining MLB postseason games are not yet on ESPN, so December/January NCAAF is slightly undercounted (est. +3 to +6 games) and MLB is 10 games (WS only). (2) The backtest window is Oct 2025-Oct 2026 snapshots; weights and floors were fit on the same rows, so the preview figures are in-sample. (3) Many Dec/Jan days are NBA/NHL-only and those sports have the weakest edge support (NHL main-pool ROI -15% in backtest, n=52).
