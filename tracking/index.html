Engineering percentiles are the stored /percentile lenses: Processes, Architecture, Math, and Overall. The morning job copies the latest manual score on or before that day. They are not computed from the record.

The track record uses the same fields as the scorecard. Record is straight bets, wins-losses-pushes. Accuracy is straight bets with pushes left out. ROI and profit include straights and the parlay, at $150 a unit.

Closing line uses the ledger totals. Average CLV is in percentage points. Elite percentile maps that average: −2pp or lower is the 20th, −1pp is the 35th, 0 is the 50th, +1pp is the 75th, +2pp is the 85th, and +3pp or higher is the 95th. Values between those points are linear.

This page only reports. It does not change a price, a probability, or which bets are published.

Win rate is compared with the break-even rate at the prices paid, not with 50%. Parlay hit rate is compared with the product of the straight prices. Closing line by price band and market, and log-loss and Brier by sport, use the stored rows. Empty cells mean that payload did not carry the field.
