Engineering percentiles are the stored /percentile lenses: Processes, Architecture, Math, and Overall (target 90). They are manual scores, not computed from the record. Each row shows the date they were last scored; STALE means that score is older than the live code version.

The track record uses the same fields as the scorecard. Record is straight bets, wins-losses-pushes. Accuracy is straight bets with pushes left out. ROI and profit include straights and the parlay, at $150 a unit.

Closing line is published straight bets only: no-vig Pinnacle close minus no-vig entry on the same line, in percentage points, with n shown. Parlay CLV shows when parlay closes are stored. "All evaluated bets" is the wider ledger (every bet the model evaluated) and is secondary. Elite percentile maps that average: −2pp or lower is the 20th, −1pp is the 35th, 0 is the 50th, +1pp is the 75th, +2pp is the 85th, and +3pp or higher is the 95th. Values between those points are linear.

This page only reports. It does not change a price, a probability, or which bets are published.
